How the campus couples them

Cross‑system interactions

The five subsystems are coupled, not grouped. Energy, water, cold chain, production and logistics all affect each other in real time.

Energy flow

Photovoltaics and battery power the cold store, pumping, the line, the dock and lighting. The cold reservoir feeds condenser make‑up and the process line.

Cold chain

The reservoir feeds cold store; a low reservoir degrades cooling. If power fails, the cold chain can breach and stock goes into quarantine.

Production & logistics

The line converts raw material into cartons that transfer into cold-store stock. Trucks replenish raw material and remove finished goods.

Scenario presets

Normal, Peak Production, Grid Outage/Island, Heat Wave, Night Shift and Storm Front presets change clock, cloud cover, ambient temperature, island mode, arrival rate and production target.
