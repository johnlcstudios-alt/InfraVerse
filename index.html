The Program

Engineering education, rendered as a working plant

Built by five student groups and unified into a single desktop program, InfraVerse is both a teaching simulator and a credible operations twin: real physics, real control logic, real alarm rules, and state that survives between sessions.

USE-06Platform-level scenario

Integrated Use-Case Scenario

One operating campus containing all five facilities as districts, joined by roads, an overhead cable tray carrying power and a process water main, with a central control centre. A scenario runner moves several subsystems at once — Normal, Peak Production, Grid Outage / Island, Heat Wave, Night Shift and Storm Front — and fifteen cross-system alarms keep the site honest.

Cross-system behaviour

Coupled, not just grouped

The integrated scenario is the point of the whole program. A decision in one district propagates through the others, and every coupling is driven by the facility's own simulation state rather than scripted animation.

Energy

Priority load bus

PV and battery serve the site in priority order. Low battery sheds control centre → cold store → pumping → line → dock → lighting.

Water

Cooling depends on water

The reservoir supplies condenser make-up and the process line. A dry reservoir degrades cooling and can trigger a cold-chain excursion.

Production

Line feeds the store

Raw material feeds the sorting line; a starved line raises a starvation alarm. Produced cartons transfer into cold-store stock.

Logistics

Trucks close the loop

Inbound trucks replenish raw material, outbound dispatch draws finished stock down, and dock queueing raises congestion alarms.

Shared platform

Everything an operator expects

The same verified behaviour is available in every facility, so learning one twin teaches the whole platform.

Simulation control

Start, pause, resume, stop, restart and reset with 0.25×–10× speed, a simulated clock and manual/automatic modes.

Universal 3D controls

Orbit, pan and zoom, double-click focus, isometric/top/front/side presets, fit-to-view, fullscreen and camera collision safety.

Visibility manager

Hide walls, roof, ceiling, equipment or individual layers, plus X-ray, transparent walls and isolated-object modes. Hiding never pauses the engine.

Object inspectors

Inspect and operate doors, gates, machines, pumps, valves, conveyors, bins, vehicles, bays, panels, batteries and stock items.

Alarms and analytics

Severity, acknowledgement, history, per-rule thresholds and optional sound; 2-second sampling, trend charts, statistics and CSV export.

Save, export, import

Per-facility JSON configuration, platform-wide export and import, plus a one-click reset to defaults when experiments go wrong.

OPS-07 · Sixth application

TWIN OPS: Field Engineer

A first-person operations game that runs on the actual digital twins. The five real facility applications are instantiated headless — each builds its exact 3D model, loads its saved configuration and runs its own simulation engine. Nothing is re-modelled or re-simulated.

Program → game

Twins seed from their persisted configuration; a co-open control room publishes live sensor and alarm snapshots every 0.2 s.

Game → program

Panel changes, repairs and citations drive the twin's own API and save path, then mirror live onto any open control room.

Work orders come from the twins' own alarm thresholds; an incident director injects real faults; XP and credits track the shift. Ships embedded in the launcher and as a standalone TwinOps.exe.
